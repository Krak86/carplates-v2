# Prozorro.Sale vehicle auctions (plan)

Drafted 2026-10-31. **Status: in progress (steps 1-3, 5, 6, 6b, 7, 8 done; 4, 9 open).** Extends the SETAM auctions work in `docs/garpun-auctions-plan.md` (its "Later" step 12). Nothing is written yet except this plan.

## Findings (verified 2026-10-31, live probes)

- **API:** `https://procedure.prozorro.sale/api/search/byDateModified/<ISO date>?limit=100` returns full auction objects, no key. Page by passing the last `dateModified` (cut to 26 chars, no `Z`) as the next date. Feed starts April 2017. `public.api.openprocurement.org/api/2.5/auctions` is 404: do not use it. The feed has no count endpoint and no server-side filter (a `sellingMethod` query param was ignored).
- **Rate:** about 700 modified auctions a day in 2026 (330-670 on sampled days, 137-174 in 2023-24). October 2026: 22 674 records, 22 445 unique auctions, 365 vehicle-like, 245 with a VIN or plate in the text. Requests answer in 160-280 ms; parallel requests to the tenders API gave 429, so crawl sequentially with retry.
- **Same data as SETAM? No.** Different platform and IDs. Only 6 vehicles (7 auctions) of October overlap with `registry.auction_lots`, all bankruptcy cases (`bankRuptcy-*`), listed at different times and prices, so they are separate auctions of one vehicle, not duplicates. Whether a Prozorro auction and a SETAM lot are ever the same sale is **not** known.
- **Seller mix (Oct, vehicle-like):** customs resales `basicSell-*` 157, bankruptcy ~100, alienation 52, commercial 32, privatization 10, leases 10. `owner` is the electronic platform (e.g. `unitender.com.ua`), not the seller. **The seller is in `sellingEntity`** (see "Seller and tag fields" below); the earlier note that `relatedOrganizations` was empty is irrelevant, that field is not used.
- **Fields per vehicle item:** `itemProps` (`itemPropsType: vehicle`): `identificationNumber`, `brand`, `model`, `productionYear`, `color`, `kilometrage`, `fuelType`, `engineCapacity`, `transmission`, `condition`, `damagePresence`, `damagedDescription` (not every auction has all of them; a commercial lot had only year and damage). Also `address.region` + locality, `location` lat/lon, CAV code `34...`. Auction level: `value`, `minimalStep`, `guarantee`, `tenderPeriod`, `auctionPeriod.startDate`, `status`, `awards[]`, `documents[]`, `description` (carries the case number and liquidator for bankruptcy).
- **Photos:** 210 of 216 vehicle-like auctions had images, avg 7.5 (`documentType: illustration`, `image/*`, mostly JPEG). Public URL `https://procedure.prozorro.sale/api/documents/public/<id>` returned 200 `image/jpeg` ~250 KB without login. URL lifetime not checked.
- **Plate is free text only.** VIN has a structured field (`identificationNumber`, can carry a trailing space), plate does not; the Volvo has it in the description, the Renault's plate was not in the text printed.
- **Link:** `https://prozorro.sale/auction/<auctionId>/` with the **full ID incl. prefix** (`BRE001-...`). A dropped prefix gives a 404 page.
- **Estimated size:** 700-900k feed records in total (the 2017-2022 volume is not sampled), 5 000-15 000 vehicle auctions with a plate or VIN. Backfill 30-60 min once; the daily run is under 10 requests.

## Seller and tag fields (verified 2026-10-31, sample of 7 days: 8, 14, 16, 22, 28 Oct, 8 and 16 Sep, 5 300 feed records, 88 vehicle-like auctions)

Method: sequential crawl of `byDateModified` over those days, vehicle-like = `itemPropsType: vehicle` or CAV `341`-`343`. Small sample: shares are indicative, not totals. The feed returns a bare JSON **array**, and the auction key is **`auctionId`** (there is no `id`; `_id` is internal).

- **`sellingEntity`** = the real seller, on 87 of 88: `name.uk_UA`, `identifier.id` (EDRPOU, for arbitration managers a tax number), `identifier.scheme` (`UA-EDR`), `address` (region, locality), `contactPoint` (name, phone, email). Missing on lease lots (`legitimatePropertyLease`) and some others. This is what makes a seller or **bank filter** possible, and it is stable (an EDRPOU, not free text).
- **`metaInfo`** = Prozorro's own tags, on every record, three arrays:
  - `directions` (sample): `otherAssets` 52, `bankRuptcy` 16, `alienation` 9, `legitimatePropertyLease` 5, `commercialSell` 4, `arma` 2.
  - `categories` (sample): `cars` 60, `transportSale` 23, `customs` 8, `transportLease` 5, `arma` 2, `npl` 1. Seen on non-vehicle lots: `privatbank`, `nbu`, `realEstateSale`, `commercialSale`, `financialAssets`, `agriLandLease`, etc. One lot carries several (e.g. `customs` + `cars`, `npl` + `cars`).
  - `auctionTypes`: `english` 83, `dutch` 3, `withoutAuction` 1, `priorityEnglish` 1.
- **`sellingMethod`** (sample): `basicSell-english` 51, `bankRuptcy-english` 13, `alienation-english` 9, `legitimatePropertyLease-english` 4, `commercialSell-english` 4, `bankRuptcy-dutch` 2, `armaProperty-english` 2, `bankRuptcy-withoutAuction` 1, `basicSell-dutch` 1, `legitimatePropertyLease-priorityEnglish` 1. `armaProperty-*` is **ARMA** (seized corruption assets), also tagged `arma`.
- **Banks as sellers work, but vehicles from banks are rare:**
  - Over 5 300 records, bank sellers (name contains БАНК) appeared in about 30 groups, almost all real estate, claims and equipment: Oschadbank (EDRPOU `00032129`, tag `npl`), Ukrgasbank (`23697280`, `npl`), Sense Bank (`23494714`), Raiffeisen Bank (`14305909`), Ukreximbank (`00032112`, `npl`), OKSI Bank, Taskombank, Bank Credit Dnipro, National Bank of Ukraine (`00032106`, tag `nbu`, scrap and land), **PrivatBank (`14360570`, own tag `privatbank`, one lot, real estate)**.
  - Vehicle lots from banks in the 7 days: **3 of 88** (Raiffeisen Bank x2, categories `cars` and `transportSale`; Ukreximbank x1, tags `npl` + `cars`, a Skoda Superb with plate and VIN in the title). PrivatBank had no vehicle lot in the sample; its pledged cars go through SETAM, which we already ingest.
  - So a "bank" filter is cheap (seller EDRPOU in a small list, or `npl` / `privatbank` tag) but will show a handful of lots a month.
- **Keyword search in description does not find banks:** the text "банк" matches "банкрут" (bankruptcy) in 10 of 13 hits. No pledge wording (`заставн`, `предмет застави`, `забезпечен* кредитор`, `іпотек`) appeared in titles or descriptions of the 88. Do not filter by text, use `sellingEntity` and `metaInfo`.
- **Bankruptcy sellers are arbitration managers** (`Арбітражний керуючий ...`, tax number as id), not the bankrupt company. A bankrupt bank (Deposit Guarantee Fund) was not seen as a vehicle seller in the sample.
- **Seller mix in the sample (vehicle lots):** arbitration managers 28, customs (`Львівська митниця`, `Закарпатська митниця` etc.) the rest of `alienation`/`basicSell`, Vinnytsia Derzhprodspozhyvsluzhba 20 (one seller, many lots), Ukrposhta 6, a Rivne children's hospital 5, Ukrenergo 3, Kyivteploenergo 2, ARMA 2, Raiffeisen 2, Ukreximbank 1, state and municipal companies, universities, a court administration. The vehicle `owner` platforms: zakupki.prom.ua, smarttender.biz, ubase.com.ua, e-somgiz.com, sale.uub.com.ua, unitender.com.ua, e-tender.ua and others (these are the accredited platforms, not sellers).
- **Documents** seen on vehicle lots (counts over 45 auctions): `illustration` 226, `technicalSpecifications` 156, `contractProforma` 34, `notice` 17, `digitalSignature` 16, `courtDecision` 11 (bankruptcy), `minutesOfTheCreditorsCommittee` 9, `evaluationCriteria` 7. Only `illustration` is used for photos.

## Other auction sources (web research 2026-10-31, search results thin and partly old; verify before relying)

- **PrivatBank:** no auction of its own. The bank page says pledged property sold on the bank's behalf goes through **SETAM**, the bank's own property through **Prozorro.Sale** (list at `dom.pb.ua`, no API, no cars mentioned). SETAM once sold 60 PrivatBank cash-in-transit vehicles by Dutch auction. Third-party "bank cars without auction" sites look stale: ignore. Decision: **no PrivatBank-specific source.**
- **Other banks:** Raiffeisen Bank and Ukreximbank were seen selling cars on Prozorro.Sale in the sample above. Ukreximbank also sells loan-claim packages (Dutch auction, 8 June 2026: debts, not cars). Nothing found for OTP, PUMB, Ukrsibbank.
- **Accredited platforms** (UUB, e-tender.ua, UEEX, SmartTender, ubase, prom.ua and more) are front ends over Prozorro.Sale, no separate auctions. Already covered by the feed.
- **ARMA** seized assets: Prozorro.Sale since the November 2023 government resolution, appear as `armaProperty-*` / tag `arma` (2 vehicle lots in the sample). A court (HACC) once blocked sales of some of the Medvedchuk property: lots can be stopped or cancelled, so statuses matter.
- **Not available as a feed:** insurer salvage and leasing returns (no public source found), Copart / IAAI / bid.cars US imports (no free API, link-out only, already on About), Ukrainian car-auction startups (a pitch document only, unverified).
- **Prozorro lease lots** (`legitimatePropertyLease`, tag `transportLease`, 5 of 88): still out of scope.

## Decisions (user, 2026-10-31, from the discussion)

1. Ingest **only auctions with a plate or VIN** (a vehicle CAV/keyword test plus an identifier). The rest cannot be looked up.
2. **Separate table** `registry.prozorro_auctions` next to `registry.auction_lots`; the API merges them. No `source` column on `auction_lots`.
3. Daily scan with a `dateModified` cursor, not monthly.
4. One **combined section** (plate card, VIN page, `/auctions`), a source chip per lot, category chips from the selling method, each lot links to its own auction page.
5. Two sources for one plate/VIN: **separate lots, merged vehicle facts**. Show the richer value, and every distinct field once. Conflicts show both values.
6. **Photos: store URLs only** (`photo_urls jsonb`, no local copies, no download). Show as many as possible: a few thumbnails first (about 4), then a "show more" control that reveals the rest. Hotlinked from Prozorro, so a failed image hides itself instead of leaving a broken box.
7. **Lots list: same pattern as now.** The few newest lots first and "show more" for the rest, per plate or VIN. Lots are grouped into **subcategories by auction type** (customs, bankruptcy, privatization, state property, SETAM seized property, other), each with its lot count. **The group with the latest lot is open by default and that lot is shown with its latest status**; the other groups are collapsed to a header (type, lot count, latest status), and open on a click. Within a group, newest lot first.

### Proposed after the tag check (needs the user's yes)

8. **Filters and chips from Prozorro's own fields, not from text:** `seller_kind` derived at ingest from `sellingEntity` + `metaInfo`: `bank` (seller EDRPOU in a maintained list, or tag `npl` / `privatbank`), `customs`, `bankruptcy` (`directions` has `bankRuptcy`), `arma` (tag `arma`), `state` (other `alienation`), `commercial`, `lease`. Store the raw `seller_name`, `seller_edrpou`, `categories`, `directions`, `auction_type` too, so the derivation can be fixed without a re-crawl. Show the **seller name** on the lot (e.g. "Raiffeisen Bank", "Львівська митниця"), which the SETAM lots do not have. Auction type chip: `english`, `dutch` (price falls), `withoutAuction`.
9. **Bank list is data, not code:** a small `BANK_EDRPOU` map in `scripts/src/prozorro-parse.ts` (Oschadbank `00032129`, Ukrgasbank `23697280`, Sense Bank `23494714`, Raiffeisen `14305909`, Ukreximbank `00032112`, PrivatBank `14360570`, OKSI `09306278`, Taskombank `09806443`), plus the `npl` tag as a catch-all. The backfill report lists distinct seller names containing БАНК so the map can be completed.

## What "ARMA" is

ARMA = the **National Agency of Ukraine for Detection, Tracing and Management of Assets Derived from Corruption and Other Crimes** (Національне агентство України з питань виявлення, розшуку та управління активами, одержаними від корупційних та інших злочинів), EDRPOU `41037901`. It manages assets seized in criminal cases (cars, real estate, shares) and, since the November 2023 government resolution, sells them on Prozorro.Sale (method `armaProperty-*`, tag `arma`). Not the same as SETAM (bailiffs, enforcement of court debts), customs (confiscated at the border) or bankruptcy (liquidator). Lots can be stopped by a court (HACC blocked part of the Medvedchuk sales), so status handling matters.
